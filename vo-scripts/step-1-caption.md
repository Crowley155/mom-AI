# Step 1 — Intro

A car is more than the sum of its parts. The engine provides power. The steering provides direction. The fuel keeps it going. But none of these matter in isolation. It's only when every component operates at its best — and in concert with every other — that the machine performs the way it was designed to.

The same is true for the Finance Department. Each function on this team is a precision-engineered part of something larger. When one part fails, the whole system feels it. When every part is firing, the department moves with purpose, speed, and confidence.

Let's look under the hood.
